The Challenge

HealthFirst Hospitals, a 5-speciality hospital in Pune, had an outdated website with no appointment booking, slow speed and zero SEO. They were losing patients to competitors with better online presence.

Our Solution

  • Complete website redesign with modern healthcare UI
  • Online appointment booking integration
  • Doctor profile pages with specialisation pages
  • Page speed optimised to 1.8s (was 9s)
  • Full on-page and local SEO implementation
  • Google Maps integration and GMB optimisation
  • WhatsApp chat integration for appointment booking
