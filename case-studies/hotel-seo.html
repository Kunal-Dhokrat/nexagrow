The Challenge

The Grand Palace Hotel, a 4-star property in Juhu, Mumbai, was struggling with digital visibility. Despite having an excellent property, they were:

  • Ranking on page 5 for key terms like "5 star hotel Mumbai"
  • Getting 95% of bookings through OTAs (paying 20–25% commission)
  • Google Business Profile not verified and incomplete
  • Only 12 Google reviews with a 3.8 rating
  • Website loading in 8+ seconds on mobile

Our Strategy

We developed a comprehensive 5-month digital growth plan focused on three pillars: Technical Foundation, Content Authority and Local Dominance.

Month 1

Technical SEO & Website Speed

Fixed Core Web Vitals, reduced page load from 8s to 1.9s, fixed crawl errors, added schema markup and set up proper URL structure.

Month 2

Google Business Profile Overhaul

Verified GMB, added 200+ photos, created services list, set up Q&A, posted weekly updates and launched review generation campaign.

Month 3–4

Content & Link Building

Published 12 location-specific landing pages, 8 blog posts targeting long-tail keywords, and built 35 high-authority backlinks.

Month 5

Local Domination & Scaling

Expanded to 30+ keyword rankings, optimised for "near me" searches and launched email re-marketing for past guests.
