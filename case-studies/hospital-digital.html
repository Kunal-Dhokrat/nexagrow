The Challenge

CareFirst Multispecialty Hospital is a 120-bed facility in Pune offering 15+ specialties including cardiology, orthopaedics, and gynaecology. Despite having experienced doctors and modern facilities, they were struggling to attract new patients in an increasingly competitive market.

Their digital presence was minimal: a basic website with no SEO, a neglected Google Business Profile with a 3.8 rating and no photos, and zero social media presence. 80% of their patients came through word-of-mouth referrals — a channel that was not scalable.

  • Website ranking on page 4+ for all relevant keywords
  • Only 23 Google reviews with a 3.8 average rating
  • No doctor profiles or speciality pages on website
  • No strategy to capture digital patient inquiries
  • Competitors dominating Google Maps for all relevant searches

Our Strategy

Month 1–2 — Digital Foundation: Rebuilt the hospital website with SEO architecture — individual pages for each speciality and each doctor. Added LocalBusiness and MedicalOrganization schema markup. Fully optimised the Google Business Profile with 200+ photos, accurate categories, and complete services.

Month 2–3 — Reviews & Reputation: Implemented a systematic review generation programme — after each patient discharge, a WhatsApp message was sent with a direct review link. Trained front desk staff on reputation management. Went from 23 to 187 reviews with a 4.9 average rating.

Month 3–4 — SEO & Content: Published weekly health blog posts targeting local and speciality keywords. Secured 45+ local citations and healthcare directory listings. Implemented link building through doctor interviews in local media.

Month 4–6 — Ads & Lead Generation: Launched Google Search Ads for high-intent keywords ("cardiologist in Pune", "maternity hospital Pune"). Set up WhatsApp chatbot for appointment booking. Launched Meta Ads targeting Pune families for health checkup packages.
